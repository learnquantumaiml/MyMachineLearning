## This code builds a complete customer-churn prediction system.

- It creates fake customer data, including age, contract type, charges, and customer duration.
- It cleans the data by filling missing values and converting text categories into numbers.
- It trains a Logistic Regression model to learn which customers may leave.
- It tests the model on unseen customers and predicts whether new customers are likely to churn.
- The Pipeline automatically performs preprocessing and prediction in the correct order, which is a recommended scikit-learn approach.

What we can achieve from this?
1) Identify customers who may cancel their subscription.
2) Contact high-risk customers with discounts or better offers.
3) Automate predictions for many customers.
4) Build a reusable and production-friendly ML workflow.
5) Avoid errors when data contains missing values or new contract categories.

Example:

text
Customer 1: young, short-term contract, high charges
Prediction: LIKELY TO CHURN

Customer 2: long-term customer, two-year contract, lower charges
Prediction: likely to stay

- In the above example, the model’s accuracy tells us how often it correctly predicts churn or no churn on customers it has not seen before.
- However, because this example uses synthetic data, it is mainly for learning; real business data is needed for reliable decisions.

Output:

Test accuracy: 88.12%
(This means the pipeline correctly predicted churn/no-churn for 88% of customers it had never seen before.)

New customer 1: LIKELY TO CHURN
New customer 2: likely to stay
